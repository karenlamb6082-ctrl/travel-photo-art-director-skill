# Art modes

Choose a mode after inspecting the source. A series may mix modes.

## Original-Led

Use when the photograph already has strong light, weather, geometry, or color.

- Preserve roughly 55–90% recognizably photographic material.
- Improve crop, angle, tone, and distractions first.
- Add one restrained editorial intervention: paper seam, type, contour line, translucent field, or subtle print texture.
- Keep the source as the visual authority.

Avoid turning a strong photograph into a generic illustration or covering it with decorative effects.

## Distilled

Use when the semantic nucleus or emotional relationship is stronger than the literal frame.

- Preserve two to four source anchors as newly illustrated forms.
- Retain no photographic pixels.
- Remove 65–90% of descriptive reality.
- Build one proposition, one central tension, and one source-derived metaphor.
- Use one primary illustration grammar and at most one supporting grammar.

Useful grammars include cut-paper mass, dry-print silhouette, broken contour, rhythm field, fragment stack, or directional drift.

Avoid literal tracing, generic symbolism, cute illustration, and unsupported surreal objects.

## Hybrid Mixed-Media

Use when the user values both the original photograph and an authored art object.

- Preserve roughly 20–65% recognizably photographic material.
- Use one photographic window, strip, fragment group, or continuous anchor.
- Cross its boundary with one primary tactile transformation.
- Add no more than two supporting processes.
- Make boundaries perform meaning: rupture, continuation, pressure, memory, weather, or passage.

Examples:

- photographic fog forest + wax-relief sky + stitched red line;
- real river strip + cast-resin stones + letterpress type;
- photographic meadow + porcelain cloud relief + torn cobalt paper;
- real flowers + radiograph extensions + risograph color blocks;
- real sunset + suspended textile light + blind embossing.

Avoid a scrapbook of unrelated effects. Every material must originate from a source property.

## Series balance

For nine images, use a deliberate distribution such as:

- 3 Original-Led / 2 Distilled / 4 Hybrid;
- 2 Original-Led / 3 Distilled / 4 Hybrid;
- 1 Original-Led / 2 Distilled / 6 Hybrid when the user explicitly prefers mixed media.

Do not repeat the same layout family more than three times. Do not repeat the same primary material more than twice unless it is the series signature.
