# Quality gates

## Individual image

Confirm:

- the source subject or distilled idea is identifiable;
- the composition has one entry point and a deliberate eye path;
- materials perform a source-derived function;
- the crop and angle improve the source;
- text is correct, necessary, and legible at its intended scale;
- no unintended person, food, vehicle, sign, license plate, logo, watermark, UI, date, or page number appears;
- the output ratio and resolution match the brief;
- the result is saved outside the generation cache.

## Series

View all images in one grid and confirm:

- at least three composition families are present;
- no primary material dominates accidentally;
- no source is overused;
- color moves through the sequence rather than repeating mechanically;
- landscapes remain the emphasis when requested;
- one or two quieter images create pacing;
- the series has a credible opener, contrast point, and ending;
- weak images were replaced rather than accepted to fill a count.

## Source-comparison variant

Confirm:

- every source crop shown was actually used;
- multiple source crops form one coherent board;
- corrections and rotations are subtle and beneficial;
- source side and artwork side are consistent across the set;
- the final ratio matches the non-comparison version;
- the artwork has not been unintentionally altered.

## Delivery

Confirm:

- numbered final files exist;
- the overview exists;
- comparison variants exist when requested;
- filenames sort correctly;
- file count matches the promise;
- uploaded folders were listed after upload;
- any unverified location, device preview, print result, or physical output is disclosed.
